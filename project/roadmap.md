# Roadmap

FMSAT is developed incrementally so each stage produces reviewable, explainable and
testable evidence before the next layer consumes it. Transient progress and verification
belong only in [the current increment](currentIncrement.md).

## Current sequence

1. Complete the active requirement 007 clean-room acceptance.
2. Deliver the remaining role-centric assessment obligations in evidence-backed increments.
3. Complete outstanding structured-tactic correction, revision-history and diagnostic work.
4. Complete requirement-level acceptance and traceability for existing management workflows.

## Role-centric assessment

The next requirement 007 capabilities are sequenced as:

1. Tactical Fit and tactic-specific modifiers supported by structured evidence.
2. Position Familiarity and Overall Suitability composition.
3. Candidate comparison with consistent role and tactic context.
4. Explainable Role Health.
5. Recruitment analysis built only on accepted assessment evidence.

## Structured tactical knowledge

Future increments must address:

- structured-tactic correction and diagnostic CLI completion under requirement 006;
- immutable tactic revision history and comparison under requirement 009; and
- broader role/position knowledge, compatibility and modifier policy under requirement 010.

## Longer-term direction

- match analysis;
- training recommendations;
- reporting;
- recruitment planning; and
- tactical comparison beyond factual revision differences.
